# Hear, Discern, Respond

**A Seven-Week Bible Study for Women**  
*Learning to Hear, Recognize, Test, and Respond to the Voice of God.*

## About the Study

Hear, Discern, Respond is a seven-week Bible study created to help women grow in their relationship with God and develop a deeper understanding of how to hear, recognize, test, and respond faithfully to His voice through Scripture, prayer, and spiritual discernment.

## Website

Visit the study website: [Hear, Discern, Respond](https://brad-hobbswd.github.io/women-godsVoice/)

## Study Resources

The website provides access to the study overview, weekly lessons, resources, instructor information, and takeaways to support participants throughout the study.
