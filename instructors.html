INSTRUCTOR’S CORNER Discerning the Voice of God Instructor Resources | Seven-Week Bible Study for Women Welcome, Instructor Welcome to the Instructor’s Corner for Discerning the Voice of God: A Seven-Week Bible Study for Women. This section is designed to support instructors, facilitators, and ministry leaders as they guide women through the study. The purpose is not simply to deliver information, but to create an atmosphere where biblical understanding, meaningful discussion, spiritual growth, discernment, and personal application can take place. As an instructor, you are not expected to have every answer. Your responsibility is to faithfully teach Scripture, encourage thoughtful examination, facilitate meaningful discussion, and direct participants toward a deeper relationship with Jesus Christ. This study addresses a subject that requires biblical responsibility, spiritual maturity, humility, and wisdom. Participants may bring personal experiences, questions, testimonies, disappointments, spiritual concerns, and differing understandings concerning how God communicates. The instructor’s role is to keep the discussion grounded in Scripture while encouraging participants to grow in spiritual discernment. ⸻ THE PURPOSE OF THE INSTRUCTOR’S CORNER The Instructor’s Corner provides resources to help facilitate each week’s gathering. Every weekly guide will include: Instructor Preparation Recommended preparation before class, including Scripture study, lesson review, prayer, and areas requiring particular attention. Lesson Objectives Clear learning outcomes identifying what participants should understand by the end of the gathering. Opening Discussion Questions designed to introduce the week’s topic and encourage participants to connect biblical principles with everyday experiences. Teaching Guide Expanded teaching material organized around the participant lesson’s main sections. Key Teaching Emphasis Biblical truths the instructor should reinforce throughout the lesson. Discussion Facilitation Additional questions, follow-up prompts, and guidance for encouraging meaningful conversation. Practical Application Activities, scenarios, exercises, or personal application designed to help participants practice the lesson. Common Questions Potential questions participants may raise and biblically grounded guidance for responding. Instructor Notes Important considerations, cautions, and pastoral sensitivity concerning the subject. Closing Prayer A prayer that reinforces the lesson and brings the gathering to a meaningful conclusion. ⸻ THE TWO-HOUR CLASS FORMAT Each gathering follows the same general structure to provide consistency and adequate time for teaching, discussion, and application. Class Component Time Opening Prayer and Scripture 10 minutes Teaching 50 minutes Discussion 40 minutes Application and Closing Prayer 20 minutes Total 120 minutes Opening Prayer and Scripture | 10 Minutes Begin with prayer and the week’s foundational Scripture. Introduce the subject without immediately covering the entire lesson. Allow participants to become mentally and spiritually engaged with the topic. Teaching | 50 Minutes Present the biblical principles outlined in the instructor guide. Teaching should remain focused, organized, and connected to the participant reading. Avoid attempting to cover every possible related topic. The objective is understanding rather than overwhelming participants with information. Discussion | 40 Minutes Allow participants to ask questions, reflect upon the Scriptures, and discuss the principles presented. The instructor should facilitate rather than dominate the discussion. Application and Closing Prayer | 20 Minutes Help participants identify how the lesson applies to their present spiritual lives. Conclude with prayer, allowing participants to respond to the lesson through reflection, surrender, encouragement, or personal commitment. ⸻ INSTRUCTOR PREPARATION Before each weekly gathering: 1. Read the participant lesson in its entirety. 2. Study the foundational and supporting Scriptures in context. 3. Review the instructor guide. 4. Pray concerning the lesson and the participants. 5. Identify the primary biblical truth of the lesson. 6. Prepare questions that encourage discussion rather than simple yes or no answers. 7. Consider situations participants may encounter that relate to the week’s subject. 8. Prepare to address misunderstandings with Scripture, wisdom, patience, and compassion. 9. Remain mindful of the allotted two-hour schedule. 10. Create an atmosphere of respect, confidentiality, humility, and spiritual growth. ⸻ SEVEN-WEEK INSTRUCTOR GUIDE WEEK ONE HOW GOD SPEAKS Understanding the Biblical Ways God Communicates With His People Instructor Focus: Establish a biblical foundation for understanding how God communicates. Primary Objective: Participants should understand that God communicates through Scripture and that all other perceived forms of guidance must remain consistent with biblical truth. Key Teaching Emphasis: God’s communication is not limited to extraordinary experiences. Scripture provides the foundation for understanding His character, His will, His instruction, and His principles. The instructor should introduce the importance of distinguishing biblical revelation from personal interpretation, emotions, assumptions, and desires. Discussion Focus: How do believers understand God’s communication? What role does Scripture play in recognizing His direction? Why is biblical understanding essential when discussing dreams, visions, impressions, circumstances, and personal experiences? ⸻ WEEK TWO RECOGNIZING HIS VOICE Learning the Character of the Shepherd and Developing Spiritual Familiarity Instructor Focus: Develop the understanding that recognition grows through relationship. Primary Objective: Participants should understand that spiritual familiarity develops through knowing Jesus, studying His Word, prayer, and spiritual maturity. Key Teaching Emphasis: Recognition is not based solely upon an emotional experience or an unusual feeling. The character of the Shepherd and the truth of Scripture provide the foundation for discernment. Participants should understand the importance of examining competing influences, including fear, anxiety, personal desires, external pressure, and impatience. Discussion Focus: What develops spiritual familiarity? How can emotions influence the way we interpret spiritual experiences? How does knowing the character of Jesus help us evaluate what we believe we are hearing? ⸻ WEEK THREE THE HOLY GHOST AND DISCERNMENT Understanding the Spirit’s Work in Guidance, Conviction, Wisdom, and Direction Instructor Focus: Teach the biblical work of the Holy Ghost in the believer’s life. Primary Objective: Participants should understand the Spirit’s role in leading believers into truth, bringing conviction, providing wisdom, developing discernment, and producing spiritual fruit. Key Teaching Emphasis: The Holy Ghost does not lead believers away from Scripture. Conviction should lead toward repentance, restoration, and spiritual growth rather than hopeless condemnation. The leading of the Spirit should be understood within the context of spiritual maturity and the fruit of a transformed life. Discussion Focus: How does the Holy Ghost lead believers? How can we distinguish conviction from condemnation? What relationship exists between spiritual discernment and spiritual fruit? ⸻ WEEK FOUR TESTING WHAT WE HEAR Developing Biblical Discernment, Wisdom, and Spiritual Confidence Instructor Focus: Equip participants to evaluate perceived spiritual direction responsibly. Primary Objective: Participants should understand that spiritual experiences, impressions, prophetic claims, personal advice, and perceived direction must be tested. Key Teaching Emphasis: No personal claim concerning God’s direction should replace Scripture. Spiritual authority should never be used to manipulate, intimidate, control, or pressure another person into making a decision. Participants should understand the importance of prayer, biblical examination, wisdom, godly counsel, patience, and humility. Discussion Focus: What does biblical testing look like? Why should spiritual claims never be accepted solely because someone speaks confidently? How can a believer respond when uncertain about a perceived direction? ⸻ WEEK FIVE WHEN GOD SPEAKS THROUGH CIRCUMSTANCES Understanding Providence, Timing, Open Doors, Closed Doors, and Divine Direction Instructor Focus: Teach participants to recognize God’s providence without assigning spiritual meaning to every circumstance. Primary Objective: Participants should understand that circumstances may provide direction, but circumstances must be interpreted through Scripture, prayer, wisdom, and discernment. Key Teaching Emphasis: An open door does not automatically mean divine approval. A closed door does not automatically mean rejection. Difficult circumstances do not necessarily indicate spiritual failure. God may use circumstances to redirect, prepare, position, or strengthen His people. Discussion Focus: How can circumstances become part of God’s direction? Why should we avoid interpreting every event as a sign? How can we respond faithfully when plans change unexpectedly? ⸻ WEEK SIX HEARING GOD IN THE WAITING Learning Patience, Trust, Spiritual Stillness, and Faithfulness When Answers Are Delayed Instructor Focus: Encourage spiritual stability and trust during seasons of uncertainty. Primary Objective: Participants should understand that waiting is not abandonment and that spiritual faithfulness continues during seasons when answers remain unclear. Key Teaching Emphasis: Waiting can develop character, patience, spiritual maturity, and deeper dependence upon God. A quiet season does not mean God has stopped speaking through His Word. Participants should be encouraged to continue prayer, Scripture reading, worship, fellowship, and faithful service. Discussion Focus: What makes waiting difficult? How can impatience influence spiritual decisions? What spiritual disciplines help us remain faithful during uncertain seasons? ⸻ WEEK SEVEN RESPONDING TO THE VOICE OF GOD Living in Obedience, Faith, Courage, and Spiritual Maturity Instructor Focus: Bring the entire study together through the principle of obedience. Primary Objective: Participants should understand that the purpose of hearing God is to develop a life of faith, obedience, spiritual maturity, and relationship with Him. Key Teaching Emphasis: Hearing without obedience produces spiritual stagnation. Obedience may require courage, humility, surrender, patience, and faith. Participants should leave understanding that spiritual discernment is an ongoing process rather than a skill limited to a seven-week study. Discussion Focus: What has changed in your understanding of hearing God? What does obedience look like in your present season? What practical commitments will help you continue developing spiritual discernment? ⸻ FACILITATING MEANINGFUL DISCUSSION The instructor should create an environment where participants feel comfortable asking questions while remaining grounded in Scripture. Encourage thoughtful responses Allow participants to explain their reasoning. Ask how a particular Scripture supports their understanding. Avoid rushing answers Some questions may require additional study. A responsible instructor can acknowledge uncertainty and return with a biblically examined answer. Keep Scripture central Personal experiences may contribute to discussion, but personal experiences should never replace biblical authority. Respect differences in experience Participants may have different spiritual backgrounds, experiences, levels of biblical understanding, and personal histories. Maintain respect while providing biblical clarification. Protect confidentiality Personal experiences shared during discussion should be treated with appropriate discretion. Recognize when pastoral support is needed Some discussions may involve grief, trauma, relationship difficulties, spiritual abuse, or deeply personal circumstances. The instructor should respond compassionately while recognizing when additional pastoral care or appropriate professional support may be beneficial. ⸻ IMPORTANT INSTRUCTOR GUIDELINES Keep the Word of God Central The entire study must remain grounded in Scripture. Do not present personal opinions, impressions, dreams, visions, or experiences as unquestionable biblical authority. Avoid Spiritual Manipulation Never use statements concerning God’s voice to pressure participants into personal decisions, financial commitments, relationships, ministry assignments, or other actions. Encourage participants to pray, study Scripture, seek appropriate counsel, and make responsible decisions. Encourage Personal Responsibility Participants should not become dependent upon an instructor, ministry leader, or another individual to make every spiritual decision for them. The purpose of the study is to strengthen their own relationship with God and develop biblical discernment. Teach Humility Recognizing God’s voice does not mean claiming perfect understanding. Mature believers remain teachable, willing to examine their understanding, receive correction, and seek wisdom. Encourage Spiritual Growth The purpose of the study is not to create fear concerning whether participants have heard God correctly. The purpose is to develop confidence rooted in Scripture, spiritual maturity, prayer, relationship, and obedience. ⸻ FINAL ENCOURAGEMENT TO THE INSTRUCTOR Teaching this study is an opportunity to encourage women to develop a deeper relationship with God and become grounded in biblical discernment. You are not responsible for producing a particular emotional experience. You are responsible for faithfully presenting the Word, encouraging thoughtful discussion, providing biblical guidance, and creating an atmosphere where spiritual growth can take place. Remain prayerful. Remain humble. Remain grounded in Scripture. Trust God to work within each participant according to His wisdom and timing. May every woman leave this study with a greater understanding of the Word of God, a deeper relationship with Jesus Christ, increased spiritual discernment, and a renewed willingness to faithfully follow His direction.
