INSTRUCTOR’S CORNER HEAR, DISCERN, RESPOND A Seven-Week Bible Study for Women A Biblical Study of God’s Voice and Our Obedience Instructor Resources Hear His voice. Discern His leading. Respond with obedience. ⸻ WELCOME, INSTRUCTOR Welcome to the Instructor’s Corner for Hear, Discern, Respond: A Seven-Week Bible Study for Women. This resource has been designed to support instructors, facilitators, and ministry leaders as they guide women through a biblical study of God’s voice, spiritual discernment, and faithful obedience. The purpose of this study extends beyond gaining knowledge. Participants are encouraged to develop a deeper relationship with Jesus Christ, strengthen their understanding of Scripture, recognize the importance of spiritual discernment, and learn to respond faithfully to God’s direction. As an instructor, your responsibility is to faithfully teach the Word of God, facilitate meaningful discussion, encourage thoughtful examination, and create an atmosphere where spiritual growth can take place. You are not expected to have every answer. Faithful instruction requires preparation, prayer, humility, biblical understanding, and a willingness to continue learning. Throughout this study, participants may share personal experiences, testimonies, questions, spiritual concerns, disappointments, and differing understandings concerning how God communicates. Your role is to guide these conversations with wisdom and compassion while keeping Scripture central. The weekly Instructor Guides are designed to provide a consistent framework for each gathering, allowing you to prepare confidently while remaining attentive to the needs of the participants. ⸻ THE PURPOSE OF THE INSTRUCTOR’S CORNER The Instructor’s Corner serves as a teaching companion to the participant lessons. Each weekly Instructor Guide provides a structured overview of the lesson, allowing instructors to prepare, organize, teach, facilitate discussion, and encourage practical application. The weekly guides include: Instructor Focus The primary responsibility and teaching emphasis for the instructor during the week’s gathering. Primary Objective The central learning outcome participants should understand by the conclusion of the lesson. Key Scriptures The foundational and supporting passages that establish the biblical foundation for the week’s subject. Lesson Materials The resources needed to facilitate the gathering, including the participant lesson, Bible, and optional notes, slides, discussion questions, or handouts. Lesson Overview A concise explanation of the week’s subject and its relationship to the overall study. Key Teaching Emphasis The biblical truths instructors should reinforce throughout the lesson. Teaching Outline A structured, 50-minute teaching segment divided into five primary sections to help maintain organization and pacing. Class Flow The complete two-hour gathering schedule, including prayer, Scripture, teaching, discussion, application, and closing prayer. Discussion Questions Questions designed to encourage biblical examination, meaningful conversation, personal reflection, and practical understanding. Practical Application Specific opportunities for participants to apply the week’s biblical principles to their spiritual lives. Common Questions Potential questions participants may raise and areas instructors should be prepared to address. Instructor Notes Additional guidance, cautions, and considerations for facilitating the lesson responsibly and compassionately. Closing Prayer A prayer that reinforces the week’s teaching and encourages participants to respond through faith, obedience, and spiritual growth. ⸻ THE TWO-HOUR CLASS FORMAT Each weekly gathering follows a consistent structure to provide sufficient time for teaching, discussion, reflection, and practical application. Class Component Time Opening Prayer and Scripture 10 minutes Teaching 50 minutes Discussion 40 minutes Application and Closing Prayer 20 minutes Total Class Time 120 minutes Opening Prayer and Scripture | 10 Minutes Begin with prayer and the foundational Scripture for the week’s lesson. Introduce the subject and establish the purpose of the gathering. Allow participants to become mentally and spiritually engaged before beginning the teaching segment. Teaching | 50 Minutes Follow the teaching outline provided in the weekly Instructor Guide. Each outline contains five teaching sections designed to provide structure, maintain focus, and allow the instructor to develop the biblical principles presented in the participant lesson. Remain attentive to the allotted time while allowing Scripture to establish the foundation of the teaching. Discussion | 40 Minutes Facilitate the discussion questions included in the weekly guide. Encourage participants to examine Scripture, share understanding, ask questions, and thoughtfully consider the principles presented. The instructor’s responsibility is to guide the discussion rather than dominate the conversation. Application and Closing Prayer | 20 Minutes Help participants identify practical ways to apply the lesson to their present spiritual lives. Encourage personal reflection, prayer, obedience, spiritual growth, and a willingness to respond to biblical direction. Conclude with the closing prayer provided in the weekly guide. ⸻ INSTRUCTOR PREPARATION Preparation is essential to faithful and effective teaching. Before each gathering: 1. Read the participant lesson in its entirety. 2. Study the Key Scriptures within their biblical context. 3. Review the corresponding weekly Instructor Guide. 4. Pray concerning the lesson and the participants. 5. Identify the primary biblical truth of the gathering. 6. Familiarize yourself with the five teaching segments. 7. Review the discussion questions and consider additional follow-up questions. 8. Prepare any optional notes, slides, or handouts. 9. Consider situations participants may encounter that relate to the week’s subject. 10. Remain mindful of the two-hour schedule. 11. Prepare to address misunderstandings with Scripture, wisdom, patience, and compassion. 12. Create an atmosphere of respect, confidentiality, humility, and spiritual growth. The instructor should be prepared to teach while remaining flexible enough to respond to meaningful questions and the needs of the participants. ⸻ FACILITATING MEANINGFUL DISCUSSION Discussion is an important part of this study. Participants should have opportunities to examine Scripture, express their understanding, ask questions, and consider practical application. Encourage Thoughtful Responses Allow participants to explain their reasoning and identify the biblical foundation supporting their understanding. Follow-up questions may include: * What Scripture supports your understanding? * How does this principle relate to the passage we studied? * What biblical example helps illustrate this truth? * How might this principle apply to everyday life? * What practical response could follow from this understanding? Avoid Rushing Answers Some questions may require additional study. A responsible instructor can acknowledge uncertainty and return with a biblically examined answer. Honesty concerning the limits of personal understanding models humility and encourages continued learning. Keep Scripture Central Personal experiences and testimonies may contribute to discussion, but Scripture remains the foundation for evaluating spiritual understanding. Encourage participants to examine experiences through the Word of God rather than treating personal experiences as unquestionable authority. Respect Differences in Experience Participants may come from different spiritual backgrounds, possess varying levels of biblical understanding, and have experienced different circumstances throughout their lives. Respectful discussion allows participants to learn while providing opportunities for biblical clarification. Protect Confidentiality Personal experiences shared during class should be treated with appropriate discretion. Establish expectations concerning confidentiality while recognizing that circumstances involving safety, abuse, or serious harm may require appropriate action and support. Recognize When Additional Support Is Needed Some conversations may involve grief, trauma, relationship difficulties, spiritual abuse, emotional distress, or deeply personal circumstances. Respond with compassion and wisdom. Recognize when additional pastoral care or appropriate professional support may be beneficial. ⸻ IMPORTANT INSTRUCTOR GUIDELINES KEEP SCRIPTURE CENTRAL The entire study must remain grounded in the Word of God. Personal opinions, impressions, dreams, visions, experiences, or prophetic claims should never be presented as unquestionable biblical authority. Teach Scripture within its context and encourage participants to develop a strong biblical foundation for discernment. AVOID SPIRITUAL MANIPULATION Never use statements concerning God’s voice to pressure participants into personal decisions, financial commitments, relationships, ministry assignments, employment decisions, or other actions. Encourage participants to pray, study Scripture, seek appropriate counsel, and make responsible decisions. No instructor or ministry leader should use claims of divine direction to control another person’s decisions or personal boundaries. ENCOURAGE PERSONAL RESPONSIBILITY Participants should not become dependent upon an instructor, ministry leader, or another individual to make every spiritual decision for them. The purpose of this study is to strengthen each participant’s relationship with God and develop biblical discernment, spiritual maturity, wisdom, and personal responsibility. TEACH HUMILITY Spiritual discernment does not mean claiming perfect understanding. Mature believers remain teachable, willing to examine their understanding, receive correction, seek wisdom, and acknowledge uncertainty. ENCOURAGE SPIRITUAL GROWTH The purpose of this study is not to create fear concerning whether participants have heard God correctly. The purpose is to develop confidence rooted in Scripture, prayer, relationship with Jesus Christ, spiritual maturity, wisdom, and obedience. MAINTAIN A COMPASSIONATE ENVIRONMENT Participants should feel respected when asking questions or expressing uncertainty. Avoid comparison, embarrassment, spiritual competition, or pressure to describe experiences they may not have had. Encourage genuine spiritual growth rather than performance. ⸻ SEVEN-WEEK INSTRUCTOR GUIDES The weekly Instructor Guides accompany the participant lessons and provide a consistent teaching framework for all seven gatherings. Each guide is designed to serve as a quick-reference teaching resource. Instructors may use the guides for personal preparation, classroom teaching, discussion facilitation, practical application, and closing prayer. The weekly guides follow the progression of the study: Week One | How God Speaks Establishing a biblical foundation for understanding how God communicates with His people. Week Two | Recognizing His Voice Developing spiritual familiarity through relationship with the Shepherd and understanding His character. Week Three | The Holy Ghost and Discernment Understanding the Spirit’s work in guidance, conviction, wisdom, direction, and spiritual growth. Week Four | Testing What We Hear Developing biblical discernment, wisdom, and spiritual confidence. Week Five | When God Speaks Through Circumstances Understanding providence, timing, open doors, closed doors, and divine direction. Week Six | Hearing God in the Waiting Learning patience, trust, spiritual stillness, and faithfulness when answers are delayed. Week Seven | Responding to the Voice of God Living in obedience, faith, courage, and spiritual maturity. Each weekly gathering builds upon the previous lesson, helping participants progress from understanding how God communicates to recognizing His voice, testing what they hear, remaining faithful during uncertainty, and responding through obedience. ⸻ FINAL ENCOURAGEMENT TO THE INSTRUCTOR Teaching Hear, Discern, Respond provides an opportunity to encourage women to develop a deeper relationship with God and become grounded in biblical discernment. You are not responsible for producing a particular emotional experience or ensuring that every participant reaches the same conclusion concerning personal circumstances. You are responsible for faithfully presenting the Word of God, encouraging thoughtful discussion, providing biblical guidance, and creating an atmosphere where spiritual growth can take place. Remain prayerful. Remain humble. Remain grounded in Scripture. Trust God to work within each participant according to His wisdom and timing. May every woman leave this study with a greater understanding of the Word of God, a deeper relationship with Jesus Christ, increased spiritual discernment, strengthened faith, and a renewed willingness to faithfully follow His direction.
